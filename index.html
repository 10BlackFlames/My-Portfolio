I’m a Physiology graduate with practical experience building responsive websites, interactive apps, and a full-stack learning platform of my own.

My path into development started with a fascination for how things work: the human body, the systems around us, and the digital experiences we interact with every day. I bring that curiosity into the work, shaping ideas into useful interfaces and full-stack products.

I care about clear thinking, considered design, and making technology feel a little more human. I enjoy taking a product from an early idea to a working experience, then refining the details that make it feel complete.
